Our Story

Fewer, better things.

Novera began with a simple frustration: fast fashion piles up and falls apart. We wanted the opposite — a small, considered collection built from quality fabrics, cut to last, and priced honestly in GEL for our local wardrobe.

Every piece is designed in-house, produced in small batches, and tested by real people with real lives. If it doesn't earn its place in your wardrobe, it doesn't get made.
